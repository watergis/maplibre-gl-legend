# Field

The form row of a property: its label, with a link to the specification of the property, above its control. With `inline` the control is on the line of the label.

## Where it is used

`PropertyField` wraps every control of the editor in a `Field`.

## Stories

- **With a number input**: `line-width` with a slider and a number box.
- **With a color input**: `fill-color` with a translucent color.
- **Inline**: the heatmap switch on the line of its label, linked to the heatmap layer type.
- **Without help**: a field without the link to the specification.
