# NumberInput

A slider with a number box. The slider covers the usual range of the property, and the box accepts any number, also out of the range of the slider.

## Where it is used

`NumberProperty`, for a constant number and for each output of a numeric expression.

## Behaviour

- A value out of the range is kept: the slider stops at its end, and the box shows the value.
- An empty or invalid box is ignored until it holds a number.
- The slider is out of the tab order; the box is the keyboard control.

## Stories

- **Pixels**: a width from 0 to 20 px by 0.5.
- **Percent**: an opacity from 0 to 100%.
- **Out of range**: 42 px on a slider up to 20 px.
