# TreeLayer

A layer of the legend tree: its symbol, title, eye toggle and style editor button. A layer styled by data shows its classes (a symbol and a label each) under the row, one level deeper. The symbol is drawn by the legend source; a layer which has nothing to draw is not listed, and a fallback icon is shown when the symbol cannot be drawn.

## Where it is used

`TreeNode`, for a layer row, or a group of the `groups` option with only one listed layer (its toggle then switches all layers of the group).

## Stories

The stories use a mock legend source (`src/stories/ModelFrame.svelte`): the eye toggles change the visibility of the mock layers.

- **Layer**: a layer with its symbol.
- **Hidden**: a hidden layer.
- **Classified layer**: a layer styled by data, with its classes under the row.
- **Fallback icon**: the symbol could not be drawn.
- **With style editor button**: `showStyleEditor`: the **Actions** panel lists the clicks.
- **In nested groups**: a layer three levels deep: indented, with the guide lines of its groups.
- **Group shown as a layer**: a group with one listed layer: its eye switches both layers of the group, and is partially checked as one of them is hidden.
