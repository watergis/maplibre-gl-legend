# VisibilityToggle

The eye toggle showing / hiding layers: an eye when all of them are visible, an eye-off when they are hidden, and a gray eye (indeterminate) when only some of them are visible. Clicking a partially visible toggle shows all layers. The icons and colors are the `--maplibregl-legend-icon-*` custom properties.

## Where it is used

`TreeRow`, at the right end of every row.
