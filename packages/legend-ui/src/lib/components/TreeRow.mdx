# TreeRow

The frame of a row of the legend tree: `[indent][lead][title] …… [style][eye]`. The indent is `depth × --maplibregl-legend-group-indent` with a vertical line (`--maplibregl-legend-tree-guide-color`) per level. The style column is shown with `showStyleEditor`, empty when the row has no style button (a group), and the eye with `showCheckbox` at the right end, so that the eyes of all rows are aligned.

## Where it is used

`TreeGroup` (the title row, with the chevron) and `TreeLayer` (with the symbol).

## Stories

The stories use a mock legend source (`src/stories/ModelFrame.svelte`): the eye toggles change the visibility of the mock layers.

- **Layer row**: a top-level layer row.
- **Group row**: a group title row: clicking it calls `onrowclick` (see the **Actions** panel).
- **Nested layer row**: a layer row three levels deep, with three guide lines.
- **Nested group row**: a group title row two levels deep.
- **With style editor button**: the palette button on the left of the eye.
- **Without toggle**: `showCheckbox: false`.
