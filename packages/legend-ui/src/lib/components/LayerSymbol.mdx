# LayerSymbol

The legend symbol of a layer: the SVG element drawn by the legend source (maplibre2svg in maplibre-gl-legend), or a fallback icon when it could not be drawn. The classes of a layer styled by data are shown by `TreeLayer` under its row.

## Where it is used

`TreeLayer`, at the left of the title.

## Stories

The stories draw simple swatches (`swatchSymbol` of `src/stories/mock-source.ts`) in place of maplibre2svg.

- **Fill**: a fill swatch (the stories draw simple swatches, without maplibre2svg).
- **Line**: a line swatch.
- **Circle**: a circle swatch.
- **Fallback icon**: the icon shown when the symbol could not be drawn.
