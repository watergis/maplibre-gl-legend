# LegendTree

The tree of layers and groups of the legend: a `TreeNode` per top-level row of the model. Groups are nested to any depth, each level indented with a vertical guide line under the chevron of its group. A line (`--maplibregl-legend-divider`) separates the top-level rows.

## Where it is used

`LegendPanel`, under the "Only rendered" switch.

## Stories

The stories use a mock legend source (`src/stories/ModelFrame.svelte`): the eye toggles change the visibility of the mock layers.

- **Layers**: the layers without groups.
- **Flat groups**: groups of layers.
- **Nested groups**: groups nested three levels deep.
- **Layers and groups in a group**: a group with layers and a nested group in its array.
- **Grouped by source-layer**: `groupBySourceLayer`.
- **With style editor buttons**: `showStyleEditor`: the palette buttons on the left of the eyes. The groups have no palette button, and their eyes are still aligned with the eyes of the layers.
- **Without toggles**: `showCheckbox: false`.
