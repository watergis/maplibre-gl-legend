# LegendPanel

The legend panel: a close button, the title, the "Only rendered" switch (`OnlyRenderedSwitch`) and the tree of layers and groups (`LegendTree`). See the **Tree** and **Components** groups for its parts.

A row is `[indent][chevron or symbol][title] …… [style button][eye]`: the eye is at the right end, aligned in all rows whether they have a style button or not. Each level of the tree is indented by `--maplibregl-legend-group-indent`, with a vertical line (`--maplibregl-legend-tree-guide-color`) under the chevron of each group the row is in.

maplibre-gl-legend mounts it with the `LegendPanel` class, and the stories with `src/stories/LegendPanelFrame.svelte` and a mock legend source.

## Stories

- **Layers**: the layers without groups.
- **Flat groups**: groups of layers.
- **Nested groups**: groups nested three levels deep, with the style editor buttons.
- **Layers and groups in a group**: a group with layers and a nested group in its array.
- **Partially visible group**: some layers of a nested group are hidden: the toggles of the group and its parents are partially checked.
- **Collapsed groups**: `collapseGroups`.
- **Grouped by source-layer**: `groupBySourceLayer`.
- **Only rendered**: only the rendered layers are listed.
- **Without toggles**: `showCheckbox: false`.
