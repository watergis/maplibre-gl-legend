# MapLibre/Mapbox GL Legend Storybook

The stories of the packages of [maplibre-gl-legend](https://github.com/watergis/maplibre-gl-legend), in one place. Each package has its own group in the sidebar, which opens with its own introduction.

## maplibre2svg

[`@watergis/maplibre2svg`](https://github.com/watergis/maplibre-gl-legend/tree/main/packages/maplibre2svg) draws legend symbols as SVG from the layers of a MapLibre style. It draws the symbols of the legend control.

- **Layers**: a group per layer type, with the legend of one or a few layers per story, next to the layer definition that produced it.
- **Example Maplibre Style**: the whole legend of a style as one combined SVG.

## legend-ui

[`@watergis/legend-ui`](https://github.com/watergis/maplibre-gl-legend/tree/main/packages/legend-ui) is the Svelte UI of the legend panel: the tree of layers and groups (nested to any depth) with their symbols, visibility toggles and style editor buttons. It is bundled into maplibre-gl-legend.

- **Panel**: the legend panel with a mock legend source: layers, flat and nested groups, partially visible groups, collapsed groups, groups by source-layer, "Only rendered" and without toggles.
- **Tree**: the tree of layers and groups and its rows (`LegendTree`, `TreeNode`, `TreeGroup`, `TreeLayer`, `TreeRow`).
- **Components**: the eye toggle, the layer symbol and the "Only rendered" switch.

## style-editor-ui

[`@watergis/style-editor-ui`](https://github.com/watergis/maplibre-gl-legend/tree/main/packages/style-editor-ui) is the Svelte UI of the layer style editor, which the legend control opens from a layer with its `showStyleEditor` option. It is bundled into maplibre-gl-legend.

- **Dialog**: the editor over a MapLibre map, with a story per layer type.
- **Panels**: the Properties and Manual editor modes of the dialog.
- **Controls** and **Components**: the fields of the editor and the form components they are made of.
